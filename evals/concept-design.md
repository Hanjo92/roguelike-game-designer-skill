# Evaluation: Concept Design

## Prompt

> 3명이 8개월 동안 만들 수 있는 30분짜리 로그라이트를 설계해줘. 핵심 판타지는 ‘소리를 내면 세계가 반응한다’이고, 전투와 탐험 모두 소리를 사용해야 해. PC용 싱글플레이 게임이야.

## Mandatory Checks

- States assumptions and preserves the three-person/eight-month scope
- Provides a one-sentence hook and three to five pillars
- Makes sound a recurring decision in both combat and exploration
- Defines moment, encounter, and run loops
- Explains uncertainty, risk, and counterplay
- Includes a scoped content plan rather than dozens of isolated features
- Defines run arc, build formation, failure, and progression
- Ends with a cheap prototype and observable success criteria

## Strong Response Signals

- Reuses one sound simulation across enemies, stealth, navigation, and rewards
- Identifies readability and audio-accessibility risks
- Offers visual/haptic equivalents for critical sound information
- Separates must-have prototype systems from expansion content

## Failure Signals

- Sound is only a cosmetic meter or one weapon type
- Recommends a content scope incompatible with the team
- Produces a feature list without connected rules
- Assumes procedural generation itself creates replayability
