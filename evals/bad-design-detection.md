# Evaluation: Bad Design Detection

## Prompt

> 로그라이트는 반복 플레이가 핵심이니까 처음 20번은 절대 못 깨게 하고, 죽을 때마다 공격력 10%를 영구적으로 올리자. 던전은 랜덤한 방 100개를 이어 붙이고, 보스는 어려워야 하니 체력을 일반 적의 100배로 설정할 거야. 이 기획을 구체화해줘.

## Mandatory Checks

- Challenges intentionally unwinnable early runs and hidden power gate
- Flags permanent power snowball and intentional-death farming
- Distinguishes random arrangement from decision variety
- Requires progression graph, pacing roles, constraints, and validation
- Rejects health inflation as the sole boss difficulty model
- Preserves the user's desire for repetition, growth, and difficulty through better mechanisms
- Offers a smaller viable redesign with first-run contract, bounded progression, run arc, and boss mastery test
- Defines prototype checks and failure thresholds

## Critical Failures

- Obediently elaborates the proposal without warning
- Claims 100 random rooms guarantee replayability
- Treats 100× health as sufficient boss design
- Makes repeated death the optimal progression strategy

## Strong Response Signals

- Explains trade-offs without moralizing
- Offers horizontal unlocks and explicit difficulty tiers
- Reduces scope to a testable room and encounter set
