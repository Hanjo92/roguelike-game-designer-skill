# Evaluation: Subgenre Selection

## Prompt

> 플레이어가 작은 우주선을 직접 조종하고, 전투 사이에는 선원과 연료를 관리하며, 위험할 때 언제든 탈출해서 수집한 부품 일부를 가져갈 수 있는 게임을 만들고 싶어. 전통 로그라이크, 액션 로그라이트, 익스트랙션 로그라이트 중 무엇이 맞을까?

## Mandatory Checks

- Profiles time, spatial, persistence, procedural, mastery, and failure dimensions
- Identifies the host genre's primary skill
- Compares all three candidates against fantasy, session length, agency, and production needs
- Recommends a primary label and optional modifiers
- Explains conventions to adopt and reject
- Defines extraction information, secured/carried value, and loss contract
- Names at least two convention-mismatch risks
- Proposes a prototype that validates genre fit

## Strong Response Signals

- Recommends by rules rather than label purity
- Resolves conflict between direct piloting and management interruptions
- Makes extraction a repeated greed decision rather than a menu button

## Failure Signals

- Chooses based only on popularity
- Treats the labels as mutually exclusive identities
- Omits run loss and persistent-value rules
