## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] First-run viability contract is honest and tested
- [ ] Permanent power has a declared bound
- [ ] Unlocks do not silently reduce baseline viability
- [ ] No mandatory stat-only path pretends to be a choice
- [ ] Failure rewards do not make intentional death optimal
- [ ] Story and essential usability are not blocked by random grind
- [ ] Catch-up reduces repetition without skipping core mastery
- [ ] Functional and collection completion are separated
- [ ] Fresh, median, and maximum-power accounts have coherent tests
